# Number Spark ⚡

A colourful maths game for ages 10 and up (adults included). It runs in any browser, with nothing to install.

## Modes

| Mode | What you do |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Blitz** | You get 60 seconds of quick-fire sums. Combos give bonus time and multiply your points, and the questions get harder as you improve (times tables, %, squares, √, negatives and more). |
| 🎯 **Make the Number** | Combine number tiles with + − × ÷ to hit a target. Every puzzle is guaranteed to have a solution. |
| 🧩 **Pattern Hunt** | Find the rule and pick the next number in the sequence. After each answer you see an explanation, so you learn as you play. |

There are three difficulty levels: **Easy**, **Normal** and **Brain-burner**. Your best scores are saved in your browser.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python -m http.server 8765
```

Then visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.

**Keys:** `1`–`4` pick answers · `Enter` next · `Esc` home · in Make the Number: `+ - * /` choose an operation, `Backspace` undoes a move.
